Dialect icon concepts

Each concept at 256, 64, 32 and 16 px on a light and a dark background. 256 and 64 use the master SVG; 32 and 16 use the simplified small SVG (flat fill, thicker strokes), as the final build script will.

A · Dialogue (rose, rounded square)

Two speech bubbles, one saying something and the other answering it: one config, said again in another dialect.

B · Braces to block (violet, circle)

A curly brace opening onto indented lines: brace-structured config read out as a flat, indented block.

C · Swap (emerald, diamond)

Two opposing arrows in a diamond: a two-way translation, either direction, at a glance.
